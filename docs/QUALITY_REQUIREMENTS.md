# Quality Requirements

Version: 1.0

Primary source of truth: `docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` Version 2.0.

Secondary governance source: `docs/REVIEWER_TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.md`.

This document defines measurable quality requirements for the MMSDP-XAI clean project. It does not report completed experiments or final dataset statistics.

Priority vocabulary: `Critical`, `High`, `Medium`.

| Requirement ID | Requirement title | Requirement statement | Rationale | Priority | Responsible component | Acceptance criterion | Required evidence artifact | Verification method | Traceability to design decisions | Traceability to risks | Traceability to reviewer observations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| QR-001 | Dataset provenance | The project must document the origin, access method, raw artifact location, and immutability status of every dataset artifact. | Prevents unreproducible dataset construction. | Critical | `src/extraction`, `data/raw`, `data/external` | Dataset manifest exists and references repository-relative raw locations. | `outputs/reports/dataset_manifest.*` | Dataset documentation gate. | DD-003, DD-017 | RK-005, RK-018 | OBS-012, OBS-029 |
| QR-002 | Repository snapshot and date range | The QtBase repository snapshot, branch/source, and date range must be recorded before feature extraction. | Reviewer feedback requested exact snapshot and time range. | Critical | `src/extraction` | Snapshot metadata and date range are present in the manifest. | `outputs/reports/repository_snapshot.*` | Dataset provenance review. | DD-003 | RK-005, RK-006 | OBS-012 |
| QR-003 | Commit-level traceability | Every row, feature, label, split assignment, prediction, metric, and explanation must be traceable to a commit-level key. | Commit is the approved unit of analysis. | Critical | `src/extraction`, `src/preprocessing`, `src/evaluation` | All datasets and outputs include traceability metadata. | Traceability manifest | Schema and join-key audit. | DD-002, DD-006 | RK-006, RK-018 | OBS-012 |
| QR-004 | Explicit labeling rules | Strong and weak labeling rules must be documented before labels are used in experiments. | Labeling ambiguity can invalidate the target variable. | Critical | `src/extraction`, `src/preprocessing` | Labeling document names rules, inputs, outputs, and excluded leakage variables. | Labeling protocol report | Label integrity gate. | DD-005 | RK-007, RK-001 | OBS-010, OBS-012, OBS-026 |
| QR-005 | Feature inventory by modality | The project must maintain a feature inventory for code, process, and text modalities, including feature source and training eligibility. | Enables leakage review and ablation design. | Critical | `src/feature_engineering`, `src/preprocessing` | Inventory lists modality, feature name, derivation, timing, and predictor status. | Feature inventory table | Feature inventory gate. | DD-004 | RK-008, RK-003 | OBS-012, OBS-036 |
| QR-006 | Semantic leakage audit | `bug_confidence`, `msg_has_bug_kw`, `has_bug_keyword`, and semantically target-like variables must be audited and excluded when target-derived. | Reviewers identified these as likely leakage proxies. | Critical | `src/preprocessing`, `src/evaluation` | Leakage audit explicitly accepts/excludes each suspicious variable with rationale. | Semantic leakage report | Leakage audit gate. | DD-005 | RK-001, RK-003, RK-015 | OBS-010, OBS-026 |
| QR-007 | Temporal leakage audit | Features must be checked for future-derived information and temporal overlap across splits. | Temporal leakage invalidates deployment-realistic evaluation. | Critical | `src/preprocessing`, `src/evaluation` | No feature uses information unavailable at prediction time. | Temporal leakage report | Temporal integrity gate. | DD-005, DD-007 | RK-002 | OBS-010, OBS-012, OBS-032 |
| QR-008 | Proxy-target detection | The project must detect and review variables that strongly encode the label, including unusually high feature-label association. | Perfect metrics can be caused by proxy targets. | Critical | `src/evaluation`, `src/preprocessing` | Proxy-target report is completed before modeling claims. | Proxy-target report | Leakage audit and plausibility gate. | DD-005 | RK-001, RK-003, RK-004 | OBS-010, OBS-026, OBS-031 |
| QR-009 | Identifier exclusion from training | Commit hashes, exact timestamps, issue IDs, and other identifiers must be excluded from model predictors unless explicitly justified as non-leaking features. | Identifiers may encode future or target information. | Critical | `src/preprocessing` | Training matrix excludes traceability-only columns. | Training feature list | Feature inventory and leakage audit. | DD-006 | RK-002, RK-003 | OBS-010, OBS-012 |
| QR-010 | Train-only preprocessing | All imputers, scalers, truncation thresholds, encoders, and transformations must be fitted on training data only. | Prevents validation/test contamination. | Critical | `src/preprocessing` | Transformer metadata shows fit split is train only. | Preprocessing manifest | Train-only preprocessing gate. | DD-018 | RK-002, RK-018 | OBS-010, OBS-012 |
| QR-011 | Strict chronological partitioning | Primary evaluation must use chronological 70 / 15 / 15 train-validation-test partitioning. | Preserves deployment-realistic validation. | Critical | `src/preprocessing`, `experiments` | Split manifest proves ordering and proportions. | Split manifest | Temporal split gate. | DD-007, DD-008 | RK-002, RK-021 | OBS-032 |
| QR-012 | Baseline completeness | Experiments must include code-only, process-only, text-only, and full multimodal configurations. | Required to support multimodal contribution claims. | Critical | `src/models`, `experiments` | Baseline report includes all unimodal and full multimodal rows. | Baseline comparison table | Baseline completeness gate. | DD-009 | RK-012 | OBS-011, OBS-035 |
| QR-013 | Bimodal completeness | Experiments must include pairwise code+process, code+text, and process+text configurations unless explicitly deferred with rationale. | Pairwise results isolate modality interactions. | High | `src/models`, `src/multimodal`, `experiments` | Bimodal comparison table exists or deferral is approved. | Bimodal comparison table | Bimodal completeness gate. | DD-009 | RK-012 | OBS-011 |
| QR-014 | Late-fusion correctness | The late-fusion pipeline must use unimodal probabilities as metamodel inputs. | Ensures the claimed decision-level fusion is real. | Critical | `src/multimodal` | Fusion input schema contains only eligible unimodal prediction columns and metadata. | Fusion input manifest | Late-fusion correctness gate. | DD-010 | RK-013 | OBS-011, OBS-022 |
| QR-015 | Out-of-fold stacking predictions | Metamodel training must use out-of-fold unimodal predictions or an equivalent leakage-safe temporal stacking design. | Prevents metamodel overfitting on base model in-sample predictions. | Critical | `src/models`, `src/multimodal`, `experiments` | OOF prediction manifest is present and split-safe. | OOF prediction manifest | Late-fusion correctness gate. | DD-010 | RK-013, RK-010 | OBS-011 |
| QR-016 | Feature ablation | Critical features, suspicious variables, and top-ranked explanation features must be ablated. | Tests leakage sensitivity and contribution validity. | Critical | `src/evaluation`, `experiments` | Ablation report includes feature removal experiments and metric deltas. | Feature ablation report | Feature ablation gate. | DD-011 | RK-001, RK-016 | OBS-024, OBS-026 |
| QR-017 | Modality ablation | Code, process, and text modalities must be removed one at a time to measure contribution. | Tests whether multimodality adds value. | Critical | `src/evaluation`, `experiments` | Modality ablation table exists. | Modality ablation report | Modality ablation gate. | DD-011 | RK-008, RK-012, RK-016 | OBS-011, OBS-024, OBS-035 |
| QR-018 | Calibration before/after comparison | Calibration must be evaluated before and after post-fusion Platt Scaling. | Calibration claims need quantitative support. | Critical | `src/calibration`, `src/evaluation` | Pre/post calibration table exists. | Calibration report | Calibration validation gate. | DD-012, DD-013 | RK-014 | OBS-013 |
| QR-019 | Brier Score and ECE | Calibration evidence must include at least Brier Score and ECE; ECE alone is insufficient for the requested control. | Reviewer 1 requested Brier Score and ECE. | Critical | `src/evaluation` | Metrics file includes Brier Score and ECE pre/post calibration. | Calibration metrics file | Calibration validation gate. | DD-013 | RK-014 | OBS-013 |
| QR-020 | Statistical uncertainty | Final result tables must report uncertainty using temporally valid repeated evaluation, bootstrap intervals, or another chronology-preserving method. | Variability is needed but random CV is not acceptable. | High | `src/evaluation`, `experiments` | Uncertainty report exists and preserves temporal validation. | Statistical validation report | Statistical validation gate. | DD-008 | RK-017, RK-021 | OBS-031, OBS-032, OBS-033 |
| QR-021 | Result plausibility | Perfect or near-perfect metrics must trigger plausibility review and re-open leakage and ablation gates. | Perfect SDP results are scientifically suspect. | Critical | `src/evaluation`, `experiments` | Plausibility report is completed for any threshold breach. | Result plausibility report | Result plausibility gate. | DD-005, DD-011, DD-021 | RK-004 | OBS-010, OBS-026, OBS-031, OBS-039 |
| QR-022 | Explainability validity | SHAP and counterfactual explanations may be interpreted only after leakage, plausibility, and calibration gates pass. | Prevents explaining leakage. | Critical | `src/explainability`, `src/evaluation` | Explanation report references passed upstream gates. | Explainability validity report | Explainability validity gate. | DD-014 | RK-015 | OBS-014 |
| QR-023 | Reproducibility manifests | Every experiment must preserve configuration, seed, data manifest, split manifest, model artifacts, metrics, logs, and environment. | Supports audit and replication. | Critical | `experiments`, `configs`, `outputs`, `src/utils` | Run manifest exists for each run. | Run manifest | Reproducibility gate. | DD-019 | RK-018 | OBS-012, OBS-029 |
| QR-024 | Environment capture | Experiment outputs must record Python version, package versions, OS/runtime context, and relevant hardware when used. | Environment drift can make results non-reproducible. | High | `src/utils`, `experiments` | Environment file exists in run artifacts. | Environment manifest | Reproducibility gate. | DD-019 | RK-018 | OBS-029 |
| QR-025 | Thesis-paper-code consistency | Claims in thesis, paper, docs, and code must be traceable to the same decisions, experiments, and evidence. | Prevents architecture-experiment and manuscript-code mismatch. | Critical | `docs/`, `paper/`, `experiments` | Consistency review finds no conflicting claims. | Consistency checklist | Thesis-paper-code consistency gate. | DD-001, DD-021 | RK-019 | OBS-011, OBS-034, OBS-039 |
| QR-026 | External-validity decision | The project must explicitly record whether external validation is included, deferred, or out of scope for a given paper or thesis milestone. | Reviewer requests for more datasets must be handled without scope creep. | Medium | `experiments`, `docs/`, `paper/` | External validation decision record exists. | External validation decision memo | External-validity decision gate. | DD-016 | RK-011 | OBS-027, OBS-036, OBS-040 |
| QR-027 | Confidentiality and release controls | Reference documents and non-release artifacts must not be exposed publicly unless explicitly approved; release artifacts must be curated. | Protects confidential or draft source material. | High | repository governance, `docs/`, `reference/` | Release checklist excludes protected reference files unless approved. | Release checklist | Release and confidentiality gate. | DD-017, DD-019 | RK-020 | OBS-029 |
| QR-028 | Baseline justification | Every algorithm included in an experiment must have a documented rationale tied to the research questions or literature. | Prevents arbitrary reviewer-driven algorithm counts. | High | `src/models`, `experiments`, `docs/` | Baseline rationale table exists. | Baseline rationale memo | Baseline completeness gate. | DD-015 | RK-010 | OBS-033, OBS-037, OBS-038 |
| QR-029 | Editorial quality independent of journal format | Manuscripts must be free of duplicates, placeholders, unclear captions, and unsupported claims independent of target journal. | Improves thesis and paper quality without mixing journal-specific rules into science. | Medium | `paper/`, `outputs/reports` | Editorial checklist passes. | Editorial checklist | Editorial readiness gate. | DD-020 | RK-021 | OBS-007, OBS-008, OBS-015, OBS-016, OBS-028 |
| QR-030 | Evidence-bounded claims | Scientific, robustness, improvement, generalizability, and practical-impact claims must reference passed gates and evidence artifacts. | Prevents overclaiming. | Critical | `paper/`, thesis manuscript, `outputs/reports` | Claim-to-evidence table exists. | Claim traceability table | Thesis-paper-code consistency gate. | DD-021 | RK-019, RK-011 | OBS-023, OBS-034, OBS-039, OBS-041 |
