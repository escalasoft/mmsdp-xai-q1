# Experiment Quality Gates

Version: 1.0

Primary source of truth: `docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` Version 2.0.

Secondary governance source: `docs/REVIEWER_TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.md`.

All scientific and methodological gates are blocking before final results are accepted. Editorial readiness may be non-blocking during implementation but becomes blocking before manuscript submission. External validation is conditional and must not be treated as automatically mandatory. Random cross-validation must not replace chronological validation.

## Automatic Gate Reopening Rules

- Perfect or near-perfect metrics automatically trigger G14 and re-open G4, G10, and G11.
- Any leakage finding re-opens G3, G4, G5, G6, G10, G11, G12, G14, and G15 for affected runs.
- Explainability cannot pass G15 before G4, G12, and G14 pass.
- Random cross-validation cannot satisfy G5 or G13 when used as a replacement for chronological validation.
- External validation is conditional under G17 and is not automatically mandatory for the primary QtBase/MQtBase study.
