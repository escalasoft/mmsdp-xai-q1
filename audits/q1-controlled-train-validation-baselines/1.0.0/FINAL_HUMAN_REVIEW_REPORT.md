## 43–44. Scientific interpretation and authorization recommendation

43. The controlled results establish a functioning predictive and late-fusion substrate. CODE is the strongest unimodal representation on average in every family. Full C+P+T improves mean PR-AUC over each family's C baseline, most clearly for XGBoost (0.721 versus 0.702), but it is effectively tied on average with the best pairwise alternative and is heterogeneous across projects. Therefore Sprint 49 does not support a claim of universal or statistically significant multimodal superiority. It also makes no calibration, XAI, verified-ground-truth, or final TEST claim.
